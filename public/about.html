Welcome to Bella Crust Pizzeria! Situated in the heart of the city at 79 Richmond St E, we have been crafting authentic, artisanal pizzas with passion and tradition.

The Humble Beginnings

Bella Crust Pizzeria started over two decades ago as a family-owned corner shop with a single goal: to share the timeless warmth of authentic stone-baked pizza. Founded by master pizzaiolos who brought generations of perfected recipes across the Atlantic, our secret has always been simple—never compromise on quality.

The Art of the Crust

Our signature dough is fermented slowly for 48 hours to develop its rich flavor and light, airy structure. Paired with sweet San Marzano tomato sauce, 100% whole milk mozzarella, and locally sourced fresh ingredients, every pizza is stretched by hand and baked to golden perfection.

Community & Tradition Today

From classic Pepperoni and fresh Mozzarella to custom creations served alongside signature dips and desserts, Bella Crust Pizzeria continues to bring friends and families together around the dining table. Thank you for being a part of our story!
